IDEA TITLE
❖ Decentralized Identity, Access & Digital-Asset Platform on a Redactable Blockchain

💡 Proposed Solution

  • Unifies decentralized identity (DID), role-based access and NFT-based asset ownership into one trustless platform.
  • Every identity, permission, mint & transfer is immutably recorded on-chain.
  • Hardened with RFID physical-presence auth + AES-256 encryption.

🎯 How it addresses the problem

  • Removes the single point of failure of centralized IAM.
  • Cryptographic IDs stop identity theft & unauthorized access.
  • Smart contracts govern who can mint / allocate / transfer assets.
  • On-chain trail makes ownership & access verifiable & tamper-evident.

⭐ Innovation & Uniqueness

  • Real chameleon-hash redaction — lawful correction without breaking the chain (genuine crypto, not a simulation).
  • Hardware step-up auth — ESP32 + RFID for privileged actions.
  • Consent-augmented RBAC — role + purpose-limited access.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

🛠️ Feasibility of the idea

  • Real 1536-bit discrete-log chameleon hash (RFC 3526)
  • 164 automated tests pass on every push (CI)
  • Deployable: Render + Vercel + MongoDB Atlas
  • Public verifiability via Ethereum Sepolia

💰 Economic & operational viability

  • 100% open-source stack — no licence cost
  • ₹0 infrastructure on managed free tiers
  • Testnet anchoring = no gas cost for demo
  • Containerized (Docker) — portable to any cloud

⚠️ Challenges & mitigation

  • Trapdoor key safety → HSM / KMS (roadmap)
  • On-chain PII risk → hashes only, never raw data
  • Single-node trust → public Sepolia chain
  • NFT/DID layer → phased, standards-based (ERC-721, W3C DID)
